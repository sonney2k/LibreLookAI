How it works — in three taps

1

Snap your clothes

Take a quick photo of any piece. The AI removes the background and gives you a clean, catalogue-style cut-out — no light box, no fuss.

2

It sorts itself

LibreLookAI reads each item and tags it — top or dress, colour, season, vibe, fabric. Your whole closet becomes searchable in seconds.

3

Get dressed

Ask the AI to build an outfit, try it on yourself, save your favourites, and plan exactly what to pack for your next trip.

Smart tagging

Snap it, and it tidies itself

No more typing in colours and categories. The AI looks at each photo and adds the tags for you — red, t-shirt, casual, summer, cotton — so you can filter your closet like an online shop.

  • Automatic background removal
  • Colour, season & style tags, done for you
  • Search and filter your whole wardrobe instantly

Your whole closet

See everything you own, at a glance

  • Spot duplicates and gaps in your wardrobe

AI outfits

"What should I wear?" — solved

Pick a couple of pieces, or let the AI start from scratch. It layers a complete look — top, bottom, shoes, jacket — that works for the weather and your style. Save the ones you love.

Virtual try-on

See it on you before you decide

Curious how a look actually suits you? Try it on yourself from a saved outfit, a single item, something on your shopping list, or a planned trip — all from one button. No changing-room queue required.
