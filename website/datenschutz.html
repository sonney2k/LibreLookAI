Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At a glance

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This website is a purely informational presence. It does not use cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising networks and no embedded third-party content that would transmit your data without your knowledge. Fonts are hosted on our own server. The sections below explain in detail which data is processed when you visit this site.

Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the operator named in the Legal Notice. You can reach us by e-mail at contact@librelook.ai.

Server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting provider's web server automatically collects and stores information that your browser transmits. This includes: browser type and version, operating system, the referrer URL, the IP address (usually shortened/anonymised by the host) and the time of the request. This data is technically necessary to deliver the website correctly and securely and is not merged with other data sources. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and stable operation of the site (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Log data is deleted after a short period.

Fonts (self-hosted)

This site uses the “Poppins” and “Caveat” typefaces. The font files are stored on our own server and delivered from there. No connection to Google servers (Google Fonts) is established and no data is transmitted to Google when loading the fonts.

Cookies & local storage

This website does not set any cookies. The only thing stored is your chosen display language, saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings. As no non-essential storage is used, no consent banner is required.

Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, the details you provide (your e-mail address and the content of your message) will be processed solely to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding) and, where your request relates to a contract, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. We delete this data once your enquiry has been dealt with and no statutory retention periods apply.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. Personal data collected on this site is processed on the host's servers in Germany — primarily the server log files described above. We have concluded a data processing agreement (DPA / Art. 28 GDPR) with the host. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and efficient provision of the website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

External links

Our pages contain links to external services, in particular Google AI Studio (to create a Gemini API key) and the app store from which the app can be downloaded. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies; we have no influence over their data processing.

The LibreLookAI app

This privacy policy covers this website only. The LibreLookAI Android app works differently by design: your clothing photos and try-on images are stored in your own Google Drive, not on our servers. AI features run via the Google Gemini API using your own API key (or credits). Data handling within the app is governed by the in-app information, Google's terms for Drive and Gemini, and your device settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20)
  • Right to object to processing (Art. 21)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@librelook.ai.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes to our website or legal requirements. The current version always applies, with the date shown above.
