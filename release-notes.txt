### LibreLookAI v1.4.0 Release Notes

#### Try-On Improvements
- **Better Likeness Preservation**: The Try-On feature now treats your frontal reference photo as the authoritative source for your face. Generated images more faithfully match your facial structure, eye shape, hair, and skin tone — additional side/back photos are used only for body shape.
- **Correct Photo Orientation**: Profile reference photos uploaded from your gallery now respect EXIF orientation, so portraits taken on your phone no longer appear sideways or upside-down after upload.
