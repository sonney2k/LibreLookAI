### LibreLookAI v2.1.2 Release Notes

#### Plan trips with the same AI controls as outfits
- The travel planner now lets you tune what the AI considers, just like outfit suggestions: a "Wear history" toggle so trip packing leans toward looks you actually wear, plus per-dimension expert item-tag controls (pattern, material, and more). Both start from your saved Settings ▸ AI preferences and can be adjusted per trip.

#### A more consistent look
- Your chosen color theme now reaches everywhere it should — outfit cards, bottom sheets, menus and pop-ups no longer slip back to a stray default purple. Outfit cards now match the clean outlined style of the travel/trip cards.
- Fixed onboarding text that could appear black and unreadable on dark color themes — tour titles, the "Your setup" summary and checklist labels now stay legible on every palette.

#### Smoother and tidier
- The restore-progress card no longer pops up on every app launch — it now appears only during a genuine restore (fresh install, reinstall, or a new device), not on normal startups.
- Unified the close/back buttons across the app: a back arrow always returns you to the previous screen, and a close "✕" — always in the top-left — dismisses a full-screen task. The camera/import screen's cancel button no longer jumps around between modes.

### LibreLookAI v2.1.0 Release Notes

#### A smoother first-run setup
- The welcome tour now walks you through everything in one place: connect Google Drive, allow background processing, optionally enable location for weather-aware suggestions, and add your Gemini API key — each step with clear guidance and links.
- New finish screen shows a setup summary: Drive, background, API key, and location each with a ✓ or a "Set up" shortcut so nothing important gets missed.
- Reinstalling or switching to a new device? The tour now reloads your saved profile and style preferences the moment you sign in, so those steps come pre-filled — and finishing no longer wipes your saved theme, language, or AI settings.
- Your saved theme and font now re-skin the tour live as soon as your settings load.

#### Outfit suggestions that learn from what you actually wear
- The wear calendar is now a taste signal: outfit suggestions lean toward looks you actually wear and have loved, ease off ones you wore very recently, and account for cold-weather habits.
- Tap a heart on a calendar day, or on a look in the wear-stats tab, to tell the AI you love it. You can turn this off anytime under the AI considerations.

#### Faster, more economical suggestions
- Fashion-trend lookups are now cached weekly per location and shared across your devices, so outfit and trip suggestions are quicker and use far fewer AI calls.

#### Clearer AI cost insight (bring-your-own-key)
- The cost badge on AI buttons now estimates the real cost of the exact request you're about to make — sized from the actual prompt and images and priced at live rates — instead of a fixed table, with a tooltip showing the concrete amount.
- The AI usage screen adds an "Estimate accuracy" view so you can see how close the pre-call estimates were to actual usage, and the whole usage screen is now fully translated.

#### More helpful when AI can't run
- When no API key is set, or a call fails, you now get a clear message — with a one-tap "Set up key" shortcut or a "Retry" for the action you just tried — instead of silent failures.

#### Fixes
- Fixed a crash that could happen when the AI composer response left out some outfit slots.

### LibreLookAI v2.0.0 Release Notes

#### A guided welcome
- First launch now offers a quick, swipeable tour through the essentials — and helps you set up your style profile and a try-on photo. Every step is skippable, and you can replay it anytime from Settings ▸ More ▸ "Take the tour".

#### Settings, redesigned
- Settings is now one clean, scrollable page: your profile, style preferences, closets, theme, AI credits, and all the advanced/power-user options in a single flow.

#### Simpler navigation
- The separate Insights tab is gone — its pages moved to where you'd look for them: the wear calendar and most-worn stats are now sub-tabs of Outfits, wardrobe tag stats sit behind a chart icon in the Wardrobe header, and AI usage & cost charts moved into Settings ▸ Advanced.
- Buttons that open a page or dialog now show a small "›" so it's clear what they do.

#### Layer a dress your way
- You can now wear a top and/or leggings under a dress. One-piece garments (dresses, jumpsuits, suits) are no longer locked to "instead of" a top and bottom — add the slots you want and they stay put.
- "Generate with AI" respects this: with just a dress it keeps the dress alone, but if you've added a top/bottom slot it fills and works around them — it never forces layering on you.

#### Better outfit building
- Selecting wardrobe items and tapping "Create outfit" now reliably opens the composer with those items already placed — including items you just added.
- The composer only offers wardrobe items that actually fit each slot, and you get finer, per-dimension control over which tags the AI considers.
- Travel outfits are labelled with the trip they came from.

#### More reliable
- Outfits and trips no longer disappear after reinstalling the app — they appear right away and their thumbnails fill in as your closets finish syncing.
- Closets now reliably finish syncing in the background, even after a brief drop in connectivity.
- Items that couldn't be auto-tagged (for example if AI was briefly unavailable) are flagged with a badge so you can re-tag them — previously they were silently unusable in outfits.
- AI features now show a clear, translated message when they're temporarily unavailable instead of failing silently or crashing.

#### Credits
- Credit pricing has been updated to reflect current AI model costs.

### LibreLookAI v1.9.0 Release Notes

#### Plan and revisit whole trips
- Travel now understands a trip as a single thing: plan a trip and LibreLookAI groups all of its day-outfits together so you can open, browse, and edit the whole trip in one place.
- Tap a trip to flip through its outfits in the swipeable full-screen viewer, day by day, with the day title shown inline.
- Each trip carries a packing checklist you can tick off, plus an editable "additional items" list for anything the AI didn't suggest.
- "Wear today" lives in the top-right of each trip day, and a try-on action is now built into the trip outfit viewer so you can see yourself in a day's look without leaving the trip.
- Trips list gained a filter + sort sub-panel, and trips load instantly from a local cache on launch, then refresh from Drive in the background — no more waiting on the network.

#### Try-On, redesigned around one button
- A new raised center "Try-On AI" button anchors the bottom bar. Tap it to open a quick sheet that launches a try-on from an outfit, a wardrobe item, shopping, or a trip.
- Past try-ons now live on their own full-screen page (reached from the AI button ▸ "See past try-ons"), with swipeable history detail and a feed that shows where each try-on came from.
- Picking items is faster: the outfit picker has a filter/sort bar, and the wardrobe picker supports long-press multi-select.
- The composer lays items out in a clean stack with the Generate button pinned in place, so it's always reachable.

#### Smarter about your closets
- AI features (outfits, travel) now default to whichever closet you currently have selected, instead of searching everything.
- Trips are scoped to their closet, items are resolved across closets when needed, and empty trips are hidden.
- The closet selector, Insights, and Settings stay reachable from the full-screen viewers and the try-on / outfit-building flow.

#### Better outfit building
- One-piece garments (dresses, jumpsuits, suits) now correctly stand in for both Top and Bottom — selecting one clears the other — and render as a tall piece flanked by the rest of the look.

#### Clean up after deletions
- A banner on the Outfits screen flags outfits that reference wardrobe items you've since deleted, with one tap to review and remove them.
- Deleting wardrobe items now tells you how many outfits and try-ons used them and offers to delete those too (on by default), so you're not left with broken outfits and stale try-ons.

#### Polish & fixes
- Fixed Generate and action buttons getting clipped under the navigation bar in the trip and try-on flows.
- Larger, more legible bottom-nav labels and quick-sheet text.
- Fixed forecast lookup for picked destination suggestions when planning a trip.
- Wide-reaching translation fixes across travel, try-on, and the delete dialogs so the whole flow is localized.
