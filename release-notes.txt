### LibreLookAI v1.5.3 Release Notes

#### New
- Unified import: a single "+" button in Wardrobe and Shopping now opens the camera for adding items.
- URL imports show a candidate picker so you can choose the right product image, with a WebView fallback when scraping fails.
- Unified similarity search across Shopping, Find-by-Photo, and duplicate-import review — same preview dialog everywhere.
- Shopping viewer now supports detect tags, remove background, and edit tags directly.
- Try-On and outfit composer flows use the new AI processing overlay for clearer progress feedback.

#### Improvements
- App is locked to portrait, except while the camera is open (so you can shoot in landscape).
- Camera always shows a center crosshair to help frame items.
- Gemini cutouts are tight-cropped and capped at 1280px for faster, cleaner results.
- Background-removal prompt tightened to keep full items (both shoes, catalog-style framing).

#### Fixes
- Bottom buttons no longer get clipped in the cutout refine and similarity dialogs.
- Fullscreen dialogs respect system bars and the in-app language toggle; fixes camera rotation crash and duplicate-sheet localization.
- Similarity histogram filter now correctly skips the black backdrop instead of white.
- Removed gray-world white balance from similarity segmentation for more consistent matches.
