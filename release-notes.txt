### LibreLookAI v1.5.0 Release Notes

#### Style → Outfit
- The "Style" section is now called **Outfits** throughout the app, matching how most people actually think about it.

#### Shopping Helper (new tab)
- Dedicated **Shopping Helper** tab with a wishlist closet for items you don't own yet. Add items by URL, gallery, or photo — same as your wardrobe.
- **Find by photo**: take or pick a photo and instantly see which of your wardrobe items look most similar. Useful before you buy something to check whether you already own it.
- **Similarity Finder**: browse visually similar items across your whole wardrobe and shopping list.
- The Shopping List supports the same selection-mode actions as the Wardrobe: create outfit, try-on me, move to closet, delete.

#### Smarter Similarity Search
- Similarity search now spans **all closets**, not just the active one, and tapping a match scrolls straight to it in the wardrobe.
- Mixes a color histogram with gray-world white-balance for noticeably better matches under different lighting.
- Duplicate detection on import: when you add an item that looks like one you already own, you'll get a heads-up with the matching items shown side-by-side.
- Repair & Sync flags possible duplicate cutouts so you can clean them up.
- Zoomable full-screen match preview unified across Wardrobe, Shopping, and duplicate-import flows.

#### On-Device Background Removal
- New optional **local cutout** path for camera and gallery imports — no Gemini call needed, faster, and saves credits.
- Review screen lets you drag a crosshair onto the item you want to keep before applying the cutout.
- Tunable foreground threshold under Settings → AI for fine-tuning edge detection.
- URL imports always show the review step so you can pick the right product on busy pages.

#### Outfits & Try-On
- Single unified **Outfit Composer**: manual creation, "Suggest existing outfit", and "Compose with AI" all live behind one + button.
- Single unified **Try-On**: one screen for previewing and saving generated try-on images.
- New **Try-Ons** sub-tab under Outfits for browsing your saved try-on history.
- Try-on detail layout fixed so the delete button is no longer clipped, and the back button reliably returns to the Try-Ons tab.
- "**Suggest alternatives**" — select wardrobe items and get AI-generated replacement suggestions that complement the rest of your wardrobe.

#### Wardrobe
- New **statistics view** with item counts and tag breakdowns.
- Camera back-press now does the right thing: cancel from the viewfinder, retake from the review step.
- Capture, cutout review, and similarity preview UX cleaned up across the board.
- Repair & Sync previews findings as a selectable grid before any changes are applied; local-cache clearing is now opt-in.

#### Settings
- New **AI tab**: edit Gemini prompts, set default style considerations, tune similarity and background-removal thresholds.
- New **Feedback tab** with the debug similarity-preview toggle moved here.

#### Reliability & Performance
- **Reactive offline detection**: the red offline bar now appears the instant your connection drops, and write-path buttons (URL/gallery/camera import, delete) hide automatically. No more stuck states after flight-mode toggles.
- Wardrobe and Shopping List now share the same image grid, so scrolling is faster and visuals are consistent.
- Foreground-service start timing hardened to avoid background-job crashes on some devices.
- Various fixes to system-bar padding and locale handling in fullscreen dialogs (similarity preview, cutout review) — buttons no longer get clipped behind the gesture bar, and German UI stays German inside dialogs.

#### Analytics
- Firebase Analytics now logs screen views and key user actions (sign-in, settings, try-on, suggest replacements, create-outfit, show-in-wardrobe) to help us prioritize what to improve next.

#### Localization
- First-run language now follows the device locale (German if your phone is in German, English otherwise). You can still override in Settings.
