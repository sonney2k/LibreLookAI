### LibreLookAI v1.8.0 Release Notes

#### Travel tab — outfit-first redesign
- Opening the Travel tab now drops you onto a list of your travel outfits (any outfit tagged "travel"), not the planner. Tap one to edit it in the standard composer.
- The planner moved behind a "+" FAB. Tap it to plan a trip; the close X (or system back) returns you to the outfit list.
- Outfits you "Save as outfit" from a generated packing list are auto-tagged "travel", so they appear on both the Travel tab and the general Outfits screen.

#### Planner layout polish
- The planner is now a full-bleed view: the top app header and bottom nav are hidden so the whole flow fits on one screen — Dates, Outfits-to-plan, Trip focus, Vibe, AI considers — without scrolling on most phones.
- "Generate Packing List" is pinned to the bottom of the planner and stays visible no matter where you scroll.
- Generate now shows a cost badge, matching the rest of the app's AI buttons.
- Vibe and AI-considers chips scroll horizontally on one line each instead of wrapping over multiple rows.
- New header style matches the outfit composer (big title, X to close).
- Empty-state placeholder ("Enter a destination to get started") was removed — the destination field already makes the next step obvious.
- The floating weather badge no longer overlaps the Generate button (it stays visible on every other tab).

#### Stability
- Fixed a crash that could occur when opening the Wardrobe tab if a transient sync left a duplicate item ID in the grid.

#### Small fixes
- The "Create outfit" FAB on the general Outfits screen is now hidden when offline (it can't do anything useful without Drive write access).
- Cleaned up a few internal compiler warnings — no behaviour change.
