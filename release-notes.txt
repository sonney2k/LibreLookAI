### LibreLookAI v1.5.1 Release Notes

#### Insights → Costs (new tab)
- Token usage and Gemini cost statistics moved out of Settings → Credits and into a dedicated **Costs** tab in Insights, alongside the existing Calendar / Calendar Stats / Wardrobe Stats tabs.
- New activity overview: at-a-glance counts of items, outfits, try-ons, and outfit wears.
- 14-day daily activity charts: imports, outfits created, try-ons generated, and outfits worn — so you can see your styling activity trend at a glance.
- The familiar Today / 7-day / Total token summary, daily-token bar chart, and per-category cost breakdown are still there, just in their new home.

#### Gemini token & cost tracking (BYOK)
- Each Gemini call is now recorded with its actual input/output tokens and attributed to the use that triggered it (background removal, tagging, try-on, outfit prediction/composition, gap analysis, replacements, fashion trends, travel packing).
- Usage is stored locally and synced to your Drive (`LibreLookAI/_token_usage.jsonl`) so it survives reinstalls and roams across devices.
- Costs shown in both USD and EUR based on current Gemini pricing.

#### Fixes
- Wardrobe item viewer no longer shows a large transparent rectangle covering the item on devices with a large system font size.
