### LibreLookAI v2.5.0 Release Notes

#### More reliable, especially offline
- Changes you make while offline — tag edits, moves, deletes, saved outfits, calendar wears, trips and try-ons — are now queued and retried automatically, and they survive closing the app.
- A move or delete that fails no longer strands items or leaves them showing in two closets.
#### Faster and steadier
- Your wardrobe, outfits, wishlist and trips now load from a local database, so lists appear instantly and stay consistent across screens.
- Changing an item or outfit updates it everywhere at once.
#### Back button works everywhere
- Item, outfit and trip details, the outfit composer, virtual try-on and past try-ons are now real screens — Back takes you exactly one step back instead of closing everything.
#### Privacy
- Your Gemini API key is now stored encrypted on your device.
#### Fixes
- In an item's detail view, the edit actions no longer sit under the button that opens them, including at large system font sizes.
