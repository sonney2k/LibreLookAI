### LibreLookAI v2.4.1 Release Notes

#### What's new
- A smoother, branded startup — the app now shows its icon while it loads instead of a progress screen.
- Long-press any day in the calendar to select it, then add, copy, move or remove outfits from a bottom action bar.
- Jump straight back to the current month with the new "Today" button.
#### Improvements
- The calendar's last week row stays fully visible and tappable above the selection bar and weather badge.
