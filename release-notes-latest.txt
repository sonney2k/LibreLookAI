### LibreLookAI v2.3.0 Release Notes

#### A better outfit calendar
- Swipe smoothly between months, see your outfits as full-cell thumbnails, and tap the new "+" button to add or plan a look for any day.
- Move or copy an outfit from one day to another, or remove it from a day — all from the calendar.
#### Plan and favourite your outfits
- Log a wear on any day, not just today, with a quick date picker.
- Tap the heart to mark an outfit as a favourite — it stays in sync across your outfit list, the full-screen view and your wear stats — and the AI now leans toward the looks you love when suggesting outfits.
#### Smarter, clearer try-on
- Virtual try-on now keeps your whole body in frame and only puts on the items you actually picked.
- Try-on and other AI actions show live progress — an upload bar and a wait estimate — so you always know what's happening.
#### Speaks your language
- Error and status messages now appear in the language you've set the app to.
#### Smoother sign-in and fixes
- Returning to the app restores your wardrobe quietly in the background, with clearer feedback when an outfit is saved.
- Fixed a crash that could happen during the first-run setup tour.
