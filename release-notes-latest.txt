### LibreLookAI v2.4.0 Release Notes

#### What's new
- Plan and log outfits right on the calendar — tap a day to wear an outfit from a full filter-and-sort picker.
- Select several calendar days at once to clear planned wears in one go.
- Send feedback from inside the app, with an optional diagnostics report.
#### Improvements
- Consistent, scroll-aware action buttons across all main screens.
- Cleaner item and outfit detail views with a single shared action bar.
- The weather badge no longer lingers while scrolling.
